# TallyVally support

TallyVally turns your receipts into a record of what you spent and what you
saved. Take a photo of a receipt, pick one from your photos, or upload a PDF
that a store emailed you. Everything is stored on your iPhone.

## Contact us

Email **alishersv.dev@outlook.com**. Please tell us which iPhone and iOS
version you have, and what you were doing when the problem happened.
TallyVally has no accounts, so we can't look anything up. What you tell us is
all we have to go on.

## Common questions

**A receipt won't scan.**
Reading a receipt needs an internet connection, because an AI service reads
it. If a scan fails, it doesn't count against your free scans, and you can try
again. For a photo, make sure the whole receipt is in the frame and in focus.
For a PDF, make sure it's a receipt or an invoice.

**Something was read wrong.**
Open the receipt and tap Edit. You can correct the store, the date, the total,
the currency, and every line by hand.

**I've run out of scans.**
The free plan includes a set number of scans. The Scan tab shows how many you
have left and when more arrive. You can wait until the start of next month, or
subscribe for unlimited scans.

**I paid, but the app still says Free.**
Open the plan screen from Settings and tap Restore. Your subscription belongs
to your Apple Account, not to the app, so it works on any iPhone where you
sign in.

**How do I cancel?**
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.
Apple handles subscriptions, so you cancel there, not in TallyVally. You keep
Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.

**I got a new iPhone. Where are my receipts?**
Starting with version 2.0, TallyVally keeps a copy of your receipts in your
own iCloud. It's on unless you turned it off in Settings > Your data > Keep a
copy in iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone and open
TallyVally. It will offer to bring your receipts back.

Your receipts are also part of your iPhone backup. If you restore your new
iPhone from a backup of your old one, your receipts come back with your other
apps.

If you had neither, there is no other copy. To keep one of your own, go to
Settings > Your data > Export your receipts.

**How do I get my data out?**
Go to Settings > Your data > Export your receipts. TallyVally creates a
spreadsheet with every receipt and every item on it. You can open it in any
spreadsheet app, or move it to a phone that isn't an iPhone.

**What leaves my phone?**
When TallyVally reads a receipt, it sends the photo or the whole PDF to
Google's Gemini AI. Anything printed on the receipt is sent with it. If your
receipt shows how you paid and the last four digits of your card, those are
sent and read too. A full card number, PIN, expiration date, or bank account
number is never sent. They aren't printed on receipts, and the app doesn't
have them.

Starting with version 2.0, Chat is answered by Apple's AI on iPhones that
support it, and by Google's AI otherwise. Each answer says which one wrote it.

When an answer comes from the cloud, TallyVally sends a summary of your
spending, not your receipts. The summary never includes your card digits or
how you paid. It can include the names of the items you buy most often, even
for questions that don't mention an item.

If you'd rather share less, Settings has two options:

- Turn on **Send only totals to AI**. Chat then sends totals only.
- On a supported iPhone with version 2.0 or later, turn off **Use Apple's
  most advanced AI**. Chat then stays entirely on your iPhone.
